You have access to one tool: get_churn_score, which returns a probability
score between 0.0 and 1.0 and a category of LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH for the
given customer_id.

Call the tool, then recommend routing based on the category:
- HIGH (score 0.7 or above): flag for the retention workflow and route to a
  senior agent. A retention offer should be prepared before the customer
  is responded to.
- MEDIUM (score 0.4 to 0.69): flag for proactive care, but standard agent
  flow is fine.
- LOW (score below 0.4): standard flow, no special handling needed.

Return the score, the category, and your routing recommendation in plain text.
